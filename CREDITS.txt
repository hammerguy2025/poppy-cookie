# Credits

## Music

- Gameplay, results and between stages: “Tea Time” (official Track 2 loop) by [t.tam](https://opentracks.com/bgm/detail/4924/track/2).
- Title and gallery: “フルハウス / Full House” (official Track 2 loop) by [Sakuttipanda, formerly Make a field Music](https://opentracks.com/bgm/detail/15849/track/2).

Used under the [OpenTracks (formerly DOVA-SYNDROME) audio license](https://opentracks.com/help/articles/license/). Copyright remains with the composers. The official loop arrangements are preserved; MP3 padding is trimmed, sub-millisecond boundary fades prevent clicks, and Unity converts/packages the audio into the game. These tracks are not supplied as standalone music downloads. Source records and hashes: `Assets/Audio/Licenses/JazzBgmManifest.json`.

Historical, no longer included in the player: “Gentle_Brew” by [PeriTune / Sei Mutsuki](https://peritune.com/blog/2025/01/22/gentle_brew/), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), retained in the local asset archive.

## Sound effects

Third-party sound effects are selected from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/) UI Audio, Interface Sounds, and Impact Sounds, released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Kenney attribution is optional under the supplied license and is included here with thanks.

The short wrong-cookie buzz (`wrong_cookie.wav`) is an original synthesized sound, generated by `Tools/generate_wrong_cookie_sfx.py` without third-party samples.

The short cookie-throw whistle (`cookie_throw.ogg`, revised 2026-09-11) is an original synthesized sound, generated by `Tools/generate_throw_pyu_sfx.py` without third-party samples. It replaces the earlier Casino Audio card-slide effect.

The NEW illustration fanfare (`new_illustration.wav`, approximately 2.2 seconds) uses the project owner's supplied `レベルアップ.mp3`. It is decoded to PCM WAV with its original stereo image, pitch and tempo preserved. Creator/license details were not supplied; this recording is not covered by the Kenney CC0 license above. Source checksum and conversion details are recorded in `Assets/Audio/Licenses/AudioAssetManifest.json`.

Detailed source URLs, hashes, and selected original filenames are recorded in `Assets/Audio/Licenses` and `Review/AudioAssetSelection.md`.

Only the Latin and Japanese characters required by the game are packaged for WebGL. The license text is preserved at `Assets/ThirdParty/RoundedMplusUI/OFL.txt`.

The Japanese and English Poppy Cookie logos were created with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool, based on the project's approved A-shape / lavender color concept. Production files are in `Assets/Resources/Art/PoppyCookie`; prompts, source history and verification are recorded in `Review/PoppyCookieBrand20260910`.
